Leadership Spotlight

Okafor C. Arinze

Economist • Investment Strategist • Trade Consultant

Okafor C. Arinze is an economist and the Founder & Chief Executive Officer of JOEL INVESTMENT, TRADE AND RESOURCES LTD. With a strong academic and professional background in Economics, he brings a strategic understanding of financial systems, market behavior, and international trade dynamics to the organization.

His expertise lies in investment analysis, economic development strategy, and cross-border trade facilitation, enabling him to identify viable opportunities and structure sustainable business partnerships across diverse sectors.

At JOEL INVESTMENT, TRADE AND RESOURCES LTD., his focus agenda is centered on building a globally competitive investment and trade consultancy platform that connects African resources and businesses to international markets. He is committed to promoting transparency, economic empowerment, and sustainable growth through structured investment solutions, strategic partnerships, and responsible resource management.

Under his leadership, the company is positioned to expand its footprint in investment advisory, international trade facilitation, and resource brokerage, with a long-term vision of becoming a trusted global brand in African business consultancy.
